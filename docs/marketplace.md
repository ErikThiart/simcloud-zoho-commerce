# Zoho Marketplace preparation

This repository is open source; it is not itself a Zoho Marketplace listing. Complete the supported private testing and review process before submitting a public merchant release.

## Listing draft

- **Name:** SIMcloud.
- **Tagline:** South African airtime, data bundles, VAS vouchers and prepaid electricity for your store.
- **Description:** Use your SIMcloud account to import prepaid products with default logos and manage fulfilment after independently verified payment.
- **Source and support:** GitHub documentation, issue tracking and private vulnerability reporting.
- **Licence:** GPL-2.0-or-later; third-party trademarks retain their separate rights.
- **Costs:** Merchants fund their own SIMcloud accounts and pay any applicable Zoho, hosting and payment-provider charges.

Describe only capabilities that pass [connected-store acceptance](verification.md). Do not advertise this alpha as a finished one-click or approved Marketplace product.

## Submission requirements to verify

- Supported regions/plans, exact scopes and versioned installable packages.
- Native installation, catalogue/import, checkout, gateway and fulfilment acceptance.
- Installation, uninstall, troubleshooting and support documentation.
- Suitable icons and demonstration/feature images meeting the current submission form.
- Privacy policy and terms reflecting the actual data handling and merchant hosting responsibilities.
- Review credentials and organization details shared only through Zoho's private review process.
- Owner acceptance of relevant vendor agreements, review feedback resolution and public approval.

These are preparation items, not a claim that review has begun. Check [official references](sources.md) and the current Zoho submission form.
