# Prepare a Zoho Commerce development store

Create a separate test store through [Zoho Commerce](https://commerce.zoho.com/) and a Commerce extension workspace through [Zoho Sigma](https://sigma.zoho.com/). Check the account's current plan, trial and widget/API eligibility rather than assuming every account has the same entitlements.

1. Use a clearly identified demonstration organization with synthetic contacts and no real customer records.
2. Select South Africa, ZAR and the appropriate local time zone.
3. Have the account owner review terms and authorize the minimum required access.
4. Configure a demonstration theme and supported payment-testing flow.
5. Keep live fulfilment disabled until the complete [verification checklist](verification.md) passes.

Never publish the store's real IDs, URLs, credentials, account screens or support transcripts in this repository. Use [the local demonstration](screenshots.md) for public illustrations. A privately installed extension and a public Marketplace listing are separate stages. Continue with [installation](installation.md).
