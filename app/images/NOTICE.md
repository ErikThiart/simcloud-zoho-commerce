# Bundled product logos

These are the original mobile-network and VAS product JPEGs downloaded from SIMcloud on 19 October 2026 for default product identification. [sources.json](sources.json) records each public source URL, SHA-256 checksum and byte count.

The extension code is licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later. Third-party names, logos and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Inclusion identifies the network or voucher product; it does not imply endorsement or transfer trademark rights. Store owners can replace the defaults with their own appropriate product images.

The connector uploads these local files as Zoho Commerce product images when needed. It does not hotlink brand images or download arbitrary catalogue image URLs at runtime. A future catalogue brand requires a matching bundled asset in a subsequent package, or a merchant-supplied image.
